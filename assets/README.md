# 程序图标

采用用户选定的 A 方案：森林绿底色、米白色伸展人物及计时环。正式素材通过内置 imagegen 从 A 概念图生成，使用实色方形底，避免透明底生成瑕疵。

- `take-a-break.png`：正式图标源图。
- `take-a-break.ico`：由源图转换格式生成，含 16、20、24、32、40、48、64、128、256 像素版本。
- ICO 编译进 EXE；发布时无需附带 PNG 或 ICO。

最终生成提示词（内置工具，无 CLI/API 调用）：

> Use the large A icon as design reference. Deliver ONE final square application icon, FULLY OPAQUE IMAGE. No alpha transparency anywhere. Entire square canvas filled edge-to-edge with uniform solid forest green (#155239), including all four corners. Center the same bold ivory human stretching arms upward inside the same broken circular clock ring with three upper-right dashes. White/ivory figure and ring occupy 80% of canvas. Exact clean geometric flat graphic, just TWO perfectly flat colors, green background and ivory foreground. No texture, shading, glow, gradient, dark patches or stippling. No A label, no small previews, no framing, no margins outside green square. Fully opaque square raster artwork.
